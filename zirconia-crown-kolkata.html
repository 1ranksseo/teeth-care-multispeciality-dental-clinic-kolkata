CAD/CAM Zirconia Crowns: Maximum Durability and Natural Translucency

Teeth weakened by large restorations or root canal therapies require durable full-coverage caps. Fabricating a customized zirconia dental crown kolkata delivers exceptional fracture toughness without unsightly dark metal margins.

Digital intraoral scans produce high-precision 3D models from which crowns are milled to sub-millimeter tolerances for a snug cervical seal.

Monolithic zirconia withstands strong chewing forces on posterior molars while layered ceramic crowns provide lifelike translucency for anterior teeth.
